5:T424,A investigation is underway after "a major fish kill" in a County Down river, the Department for Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs (Daera) has confirmed.

The pollution caused the death of brown and migratory sea trout in the Gransha, Ballystockart and Inler (also known as Enler) Rivers.

Glenn McKibbin, the secretary of Inler Angling Club, said about 1,000 fish were killed in the incident and it "could not have happened in a worse location on the river or at a worse time".

The Northern Ireland Environment Agency (NIEA) and Daera Inland Fisheries are working to "identify the source of the pollution and to finalise the numbers of fish killed".

The Department said the incident was reported by a member of the public on Tuesday at 09:30 GMT.

McKibbin said that "current and future fish stocks obliterated for years to come".

He added that resident and non-resident migratory fish, which only return at this of year to spawn, have been impacted.
