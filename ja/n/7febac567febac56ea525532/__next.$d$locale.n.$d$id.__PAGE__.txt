DEMOSO, Myanmar: Around 30 people were killed in two Myanmar military airstrikes on Saturday (Nov 28), witnesses said, bringing the bombing toll to about 60 in three days, a week after an international court found the state had committed genocide .

A strike on a wedding venue in Moe Bye district in the northeast killed around 20 people, according to witnesses, with another nine said to have died in another bombing in western Rakhine state.

The self-declared "National Unity Government" (NUG) in exile said 29 people had been killed Thursday in central Myanmar.

The three strikes are the latest mass casualty incidents in the brutal civil war that followed the 2021 coup that deposed Aung San Suu Kyi's elected government.
