He said when used in EU territory, the system worked at higher speeds.

It comes as US President Donald Trump pauses incoming sanctions against Russia, after agreeing to purchase diesel from the country.

Ukraine is now unable to use its interceptor systems against Russian drone attacks because of a Starlink speed restriction, president Volodymyr Zelenskyy says.

He said any restrictions would have been set by the US military and US President Donald Trump.

Mr Zelenskyy told reporters in a WhatsApp media chat Ukrainian officials had been told the restrictions were linked to technical issues, adding he was discussing it with US officials.
