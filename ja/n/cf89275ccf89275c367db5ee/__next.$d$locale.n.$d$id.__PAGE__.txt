U.S. immigration officials defended federal agents on Friday who shot a New York City man travelling with his five-year-old child.

One senior ICE official said agents shot the Dominican man because they believed their lives were at risk, while Homeland Security said the officers did not know there was a child in the car.

The man identified as Oscar Belgal, 28, was taken to hospital, arrested and then taken to New Jersey, one of his lawyers said.

Democrats and New Yorkers were quick to condemn the shooting, with the mayor describing the situation as "unconscionable and incredibly serious."

Earlier Friday, a judge ordered officials not to remove the man from the country while his lawyers argued an emergency petition for his release.
