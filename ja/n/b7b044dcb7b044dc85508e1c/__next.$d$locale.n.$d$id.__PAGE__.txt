5:T4e5,Italy's data protection agency said on Saturday that it was imposing a €805 million (around $887.3 million) fine on Meta for violating the privacy of Italian users.

Italian regulators say Meta accessed the photos and videos from Italian Android phone users via the Facebook app. The regulators said the privacy infringement was due to a feature on the Facebook app which suggests photos from users' personal galleries which they can then post on the social media platform.

The data protection agency said Meta did this "without obtaining specific consent and without properly informing them."

The fine, which is equal to 0.4% of Meta's annual turnover, is the largest-ever imposed by Italian regulators on a big tech company.

Meta has 60 days to disable the camera roll suggestions feature and to separately ask for permission from Facebook users for just that function.

Italy's data protection agency said there a "high number of people affected" by Meta's data privacy infringement "which is still ongoing for those who have not updated the app and for those who have already given their consent to the system."
