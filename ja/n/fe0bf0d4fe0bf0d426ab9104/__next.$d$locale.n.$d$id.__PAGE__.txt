With the summit due to close on Friday, tensions are rising as activists warn the talks are headed for failure, and negotiators search for a deal that nearly 200 governments can accept.

Countries that do not usually negotiate together stood side by side during a press conference in Antalya on Friday, with a message for the COP31 Presidency: the agreement on the table is not good enough.

Led by France and Panama, ministers and representatives from across the world including Belgium, Côte d’Ivoire, Finland, Greece, Guatemala, Spain, Sweden and Vanuatu, joined forces to demand stronger climate action.

“We cannot [tell] the world we are now getting to 1.5 [degrees of warming] and then do business as if nothing was happening,” said France's Minister for Ecological Transition, Monique Barbut.
