SINGAPORE: After smashing records at the recent SEA Games, Commonwealth Games and Asian Games, and bringing home a haul of medals along the way, everyone wants to know what Gan Ching Hwee has set her eyes on next.

The 23-year-old swimmer has had a stellar past two years. At her Olympic debut in 2024, the Indiana University graduate set national records in the 800m and 1,500m freestyle, becoming the first Singaporean female swimmer to set a national record at the Games since Tao Li (100m backstroke) in 2012.

At the 2025 SEA Games, Gan swept the 200m, 400m, and 800m freestyle events for the third consecutive edition. She lowered her 400m freestyle national record at the Glasgow Commonwealth Games, before concluding her 2026 Asian Games campaign with three silvers and national records in the 400m, 800m and 1,500m freestyle.
