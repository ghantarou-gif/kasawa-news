5:T4cd,After eight years of Socialist-led government under Pedro Sánchez, Spain is at a crossroads.

Snap elections are taking place on 29 November, and voters face a choice of continued left-wing rule or a conservative-led government, with the likely support of far-right Vox.

The polls open at 09:00 (08:00 GMT) on Sunday and close at 20:00 (19:00 GMT). Provisional results will start appearing an hour later.

Sánchez called this vote eight months early after MPs rejected planned changes to Spain's housing laws.

He was responding to nationwide protests after an 87-year-old woman called Maricarmen Abascal was evicted from her rented flat in Madrid and later died.

But his government lost the support of a centre-right party called Junts from the north-eastern region of Catalonia which sought to protect the rights of property owners.

Housing remains one of the hot-button issues for voters, but they are also focused on the high cost of living, corruption and immigration. And the election takes place within weeks of a devastating rail disaster that has shattered confidence in Spain’s high-speed rail network.
