18:T91f,Two police forces are being investigated after a 21-year-old woman was killed when the car she was travelling in crashed following a pursuit.

The Independent Office for Police Conduct said it was investigating Staffordshire police and West Mercia police after the collision between a Seat Ibiza and Vauxhall Astra in Hednesford in the West Midlands on Friday night.

Staffordshire police said it had reported itself to the IOPC following the incident shortly after 11pm. The police watchdog said the pursuit began when the Seat, believed to be fitted with cloned plates, failed to stop.

It collided with the Vauxhall which was being driven by a member of the public. The crash took place on the Rugeley Road, a single-carriageway A-road that runs to the east of Hednesford.

The woman was a front seat passenger in the Seat. She was pronounced dead at the scene. The driver of the car sustained serious injuries and is being treated in hospital.

Staffordshire police said a 23-year-old man from the nearby Walsall area has been arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving.

An IOPC spokesperson said: “We are independently investigating the involvement of Staffordshire police and West Midlands police prior to a road traffic collision in Cannock, in which a woman has sadly died.

“Our investigation will look at police actions prior to the fatal collision.

“After being notified by the force, we declared an independent investigation at 12.13am on Saturday (28 November) and sent investigators to the police post incident procedures and scene to begin gathering evidence.”

A spokesperson for West Midlands police said: “We’re assisting both Staffordshire police and the IOPC with their investigations. Our thoughts remain with the family of the woman who died.”

The woman’s next-of-kin have been informed. Staffordshire police said the family were being supported by specially trained officers.

West Midlands ambulance service took two people to hospital. Their injuries are not believed to be life-threatening or life-changing.

Police said the driver of the Astra was helping with inquiries. They appealed to speak to anyone who was in the area or saw the cars in the lead up to the crash.
