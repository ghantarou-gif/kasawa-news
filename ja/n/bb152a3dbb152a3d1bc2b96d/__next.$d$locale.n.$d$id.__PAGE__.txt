A mathematician working for artificial intelligence firm Anthropic claims to have solved one of the world's most important maths problems with the help of AI.

Levent Alpöge posted a four-page note on Friday saying he believed he had found a proof to the Riemann hypothesis by working with Anthropic's Claude AI tool.

"Claude and I have had a great time together with such breadth in recent months, but this time Claude far outpaced me near the finish line," Alpöge wrote. "No, Claude flew, and danced, and sparkled near that finish line."

Formulated in an 1859 paper by German mathematician Bernhard Riemann, the Riemann hypothesis refers to the distribution of prime numbers as they stretch towards infinity.

"This all happened for me late Sunday night," Alpöge continued, adding that he had contacted Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei to ask about announcing his find. His announcement eventually came a day after Anthropic's blockbuster Thursday stock market debut.
