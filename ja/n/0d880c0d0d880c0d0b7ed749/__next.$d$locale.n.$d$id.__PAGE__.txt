And whenever he returned to his hotel room in the evenings, he says, the same thing kept happening.

Wilder, who was a China specialist with the agency and now lectures at Georgetown University, says he never took the obvious bait. But he knows plenty of American men did.

The next morning, they would get a knock on the door. They would be greeted by someone with incriminating photos, warned they had broken the law, and encouraged to "cooperate" — often by sharing sensitive information.
