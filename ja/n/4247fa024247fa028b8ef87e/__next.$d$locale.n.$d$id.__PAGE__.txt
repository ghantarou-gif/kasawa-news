The S&P 500 tumbled about 1.8 per cent Friday for its worst day since June. The weakness of technology stocks dragged the Nasdaq composite down a market-leading 2.9 per cent, while the Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped 1.1 per cent.

Canada's main index, the S&P/TSX composite, bounced back from its afternoon drop of 2.3 per cent to close down 1.7 per cent.

Intel's stock led the way lower and plunged 28.6 per cent for its worst drop since 1974. The chipmaker gave a forecast for profit in the final three months of this year that fell short of analysts' expectations, as CEO Lip-Bu Tan said the personal-computer industry continues to feel pressure.
