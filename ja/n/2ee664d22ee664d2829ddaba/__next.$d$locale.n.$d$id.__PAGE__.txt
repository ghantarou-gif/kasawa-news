Nick Owens, an attorney in Brown County, Ohio, stands at the edge of a Mt. Orab neighborhood next to farmland that has been sold to a data center developer. Owens is leading an effort to halt the buildout of hyperscale data centers in communities like this one.

MT. ORAB, Ohio — The data center mystery in this rural exurb of Cincinnati has consumed Nick Owens.

The lifelong Republican and self-described small-town attorney has spent countless hours trying to figure out what's going on with 1,000 acres of farmland and trees that property records show were sold to a data center developer in 2024 and 2025.

In a few spots, the data center property runs right up against beautiful two-story homes.

"This is the Ohio that people brag about. This is the Ohio people love. This is the small town charm," he says. "Living in the rural area, but getting the benefits of a nice, lovely neighborhood."
