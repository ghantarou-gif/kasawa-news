Now, AI-generated voice calls are posing a new challenge.

In August, a caller made a bomb threat to Wheatland Union High School in Yuba County, Calif.

Police responded overnight and classes were canceled. There was no bomb and no one was hurt.

Authorities said it was part of a series of hoax calls — also called swatting — made to schools across the country over the summer.
