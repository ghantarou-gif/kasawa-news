Saudi authorities reported that several missiles and drones were intercepted elsewhere, and that falling debris injured four people in the capital, Riyadh.

The Iran-backed group also warned that there would be further attacks in the coming days.

The Saudi cabinet, chaired by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, vowed on Tuesday that the Houthis would "bear the consequences" of their recent attacks on the kingdom, including a missile strike on Riyadh's airport on Saturday that killed 13 people.
