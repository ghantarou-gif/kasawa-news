Iran's Revolutionary Guard says four oil tankers, including a "supertanker" carrying more than two million barrels of oil, have hit mines and caught fire while travelling through the Strait of Hormuz.

The IRGC said the tankers suffered explosions on Friday after attempting to use unauthorised routes through the strait.

The Guard's navy claimed the vessels had switched off their navigation and positioning systems and ignored repeated radio warnings. Iran's report did not identify the vessels or provide details about the crews.

Tehran has asserted control over much of the waterway since the US and Israel launched the war on February 28. ( AP: Razieh Poudat/ISNA )
