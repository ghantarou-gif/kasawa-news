The Jillaroos have continued a weekend of lopsided scores as they thumped England 78-0 at the Women's Rugby League World Cup, although the victory was soured by a tournament-ending broken wrist for gun centre Julia Robinson.

Eleven Australians crossed for tries in Perth, with Jaime Chapman, Jocelyn Kelleher, Isabelle Kelly and Abbi Church nabbing doubles in a 15-try rout.

Robinson also got on the board in the 16th minute, but her evening and tournament came to a painful end just a few minutes later when she got her arm trapped between teammate Shaylee Bent and England winger Amelia Brown in a tackle.

The Broncos superstar immediately came reeling out of the tackle holding her left wrist and was taken straight off the field before being taken to hospital.

Despite missing their greatest try-scorer, the Jillaroos carried on unfazed to romp to an enormous victory that fell just short of the 90-4 win they enjoyed in their against England in Las Vegas in early 2025.
