5:T4d3,Video. North Macedonia: Kriva Palanka seeks help over 1,000 free-roaming cows | Euronews

More than 1,000 cows are roaming freely through Kriva Palanka in North Macedonia, damaging property and alarming residents. The mayor wants the government to declare a 30-day state of crisis.

Recent footage shows cattle wandering through traffic, grazing in public parks and moving between houses , sometimes after dark. Local authorities in the north-eastern municipality of Kriva Palanka say the animals have caused extensive damage to farmland and municipal property , while residents fear unexpected encounters with the large livestock.

Mayor Saško Mitovski has asked the government to declare a 30-day state of crisis , warning that the situation has steadily deteriorated. According to the mayor, the number of roaming cattle has risen from around 400 five years ago to more than 1,000 today .

Many of the animals previously belonged to a single family , whose members were detained after allegedly obstructing an attempt by the Food and Veterinary Agency to confiscate the cattle.
