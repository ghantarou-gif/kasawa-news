Thieves in Italy took off with a €5 million haul consisting of 30,000 wine bottles after targeting a prestigious winemaker in Tuscany last weekend, Italian media reported.

A group of at least seven individuals carried out the heist at a winery belonging to the Marchesi Antinori company in Cortona, making away with bottles of Solaia, Tignanello, and Guado al Tasso, per a report in the Corriere della Sera newspaper.

The report said the thieves arrived in two large trucks and had deactivated the alarm system and disabled cameras at the site.

Renzo Cotarella, CEO of Marchesi Antinori, told the outlet that the gang appeared to be "well prepared."

"Perhaps it is the same group that has targeted other wineries in Italy over the past few months. One thing is certain - a theft of this scale and value has never occurred in Italy," he continued.

The executive was nevertheless confident that at least some of the bottles could be recovered.
