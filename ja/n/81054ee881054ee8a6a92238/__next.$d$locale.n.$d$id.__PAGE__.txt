5:T568,Rory McIlroy's hopes of winning a record-equalling eighth Race to Dubai title faded as he fell four shots behind leader Tyrrell Hatton after three rounds of the season-ending DP World Tour Championship.

Northern Irishman McIlroy started Saturday tied for the lead with Hatton but, while the Englishman remained at the top of the field with a four-under-par 68, McIlroy fell away with a 72 at the Earth Course in Dubai.

Hatton, on nine under for the tournament, holds a one-shot lead over France's Victor Perez as he bids for a first DP World Tour Championship, while Sweden's Mikael Lindberg is third on six under.

McIlroy is joint fourth in a group of four players which includes Spain's Eugenio Chacarra, the current leader in the Race to Dubai standings.

Six-time major champion McIlroy began the tournament trailing Chacarra by more than 500 points, but knowing a win would guarantee him an eighth season-long crown to equal the tally set by Scotland's Colin Montgomerie.

McIlroy also has a chance of winning the title if he finishes in the top four and Chacarra falls down the order.

He took the outright lead with an eagle three on the par-five seventh, before bogeys on the eighth, ninth and 10th.
