Kobie Dee knows the power of music as a positive force that can steer young people towards a less destructive path. He knows the road they're on.

"I was very fortunate that I didn't end up in jail," he tells Double J's Michael Hing.

"I was very much walking down a road that was leading that way. For me, it was, 'If I don't get my life right, if I don't get myself help, these opportunities aren't going to last for me.'

"There were so many opportunities that came when I was heavy in addiction, and all the stuff I was doing. I missed out on so much stuff. There were big shows that I was supposed to do that I didn't do because I was heavy in addiction."

The 29-year-old Gomeroi man and award-winning musician has recently spent weeks working as part of the Hear Me Out, Music Behind Bars initiative from the Australian Children's Music Foundation, an intensive six-week songwriting pilot for those detained at the Cobham Youth Justice Centre.
