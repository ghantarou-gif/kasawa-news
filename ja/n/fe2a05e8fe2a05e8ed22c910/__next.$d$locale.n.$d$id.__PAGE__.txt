“More than anything, they want to be believed,” said Erin Gerber, a GBV response specialist with the UN reproductive and sexual health agency, UNFPA , based in Nairobi.

“That was something that came up over and over again.”

Acknowledging survivors’ experiences and ensuring they receive safe and quality support is at the heart of Women at the Centre , UNFPA’s first global GBV case management programme.

Currently implemented in Azerbaijan, El Salvador, Indonesia, Madagascar and Zimbabwe, it aims to strengthen the services survivors receive while building a consistent and professional social service workforce.

The programme began by examining existing services and identifying barriers to accessing them.

Assessments pointed to seven marginalized groups that have historically faced discrimination or exclusion, including adolescent girls, older women, LGBTIQ+ survivors, persons with disabilities, people of African descent, Indigenous People, and sex workers.
