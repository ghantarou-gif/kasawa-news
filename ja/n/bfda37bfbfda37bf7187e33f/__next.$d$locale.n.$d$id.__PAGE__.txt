The UK's competition watchdog has launched an investigation into whether Meta is imposing "exploitative" new terms that allow it to use publishers' content for its own products, including AI, without giving media organisations adequate choice.

The probe will examine whether the "terms and conditions" from the US technology giant "unfairly harm news publishers and other content businesses", the Competition and Markets Authority said in a statement.

The CMA said the new terms, which the Facebook and Instagram parent had planned to introduce on November 20 before putting it on hold, could amount to an abuse of its dominant position in the market.

The watchdog will determine whether "Meta is abusing a dominant position by imposing exploitative terms on publishers and advertisers".

Meta's new terms could allow it to access and use content from news publishers' websites to develop and provide its own products, including its AI features.
