The vote could pave the way for a change of government, with Alberto Núñez Feijóo becoming prime minister and the far right potentially entering government for the first time since the end of Franco’s dictatorship. Alternatively, it could see Pedro Sánchez, who has been in power since 2018, secure another term in office.

Access to housing , immigration, the economy and allegations of corruption involving people close to the prime minister have dominated much of the campaign.
