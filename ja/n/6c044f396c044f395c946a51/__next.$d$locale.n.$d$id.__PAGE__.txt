5:T576,Women participate in technical and entrepreneurial training supported under a UN-Habitat project in Burkina Faso.

A home is a place where people can feel safe. Yet billions worldwide do not have a proper place to live. As the global housing crisis deepens, government representatives and housing experts are gathering at the United Nations in Nairobi from 26-27 November to discuss how to address it.

For many people living in informal settlements, the housing crisis goes hand in hand with the climate crisis.

“People don’t sleep at night because they don't know if their houses will be standing the next day,” said Anacláudia Rossbach, Executive Director of UN-Habitat , the agency working to make cities more sustainable, which is hosting the meeting.

The objective is to better understand the scale and nature of homelessness, improve national housing policies, and ensure that housing can withstand growing environmental and climate pressures.

This is the third session of the Open-ended Intergovernmental Expert Working Group on Adequate Housing for All (OEWG-H) which was established in 2023. It aims to advance a comprehensive set of recommendations for the UN-Habitat Assembly in 2029.

Speaking to UN News ahead of the meeting, Ms. Rossbach emphasized the importance of bringing the housing crisis to the attention of Member States and building consensus on how to address it.d:X
