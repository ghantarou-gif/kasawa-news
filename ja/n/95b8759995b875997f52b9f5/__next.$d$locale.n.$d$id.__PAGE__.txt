Rausch was backed by his own party and the Alliance for Social Justice and Economic Rationality (BSW), formerly known as the Sahra Wagenknecht Alliance, as had been expected. The march concluded with SPD Federal Minister of Defense Boris Pistorius leading the crowd in the German national anthem.
