Hungarian Prime Minister Péter Magyar sought to bring European leaders to Budapest for a major commemoration of the 70th anniversary of the 1956 revolution.

In the end, only European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen and European Council President António Costa are attending on behalf of the EU leadership.

Late invitations and diplomatic tensions contributed to the absence of key figures, including French President Emmanuel Macron, Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy.

Magyar had planned a major international commemoration on 23 October to mark the anti-Soviet uprising, saying the day after his landslide election victory that he wanted to welcome some of the world’s most senior leaders to Budapest.
