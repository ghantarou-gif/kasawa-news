GENOA, Italy, Nov 14 : Italy came close but ultimately fell to another Nations Championship loss against Argentina on Saturday, with head coach Gonzalo Quesada left lamenting the errors and missed opportunities which cost his side.

The final seconds of the match summed up Italy's afternoon in Genoa, putting themselves in a position to beat Argentina but letting the chance slip away in a 19-14 defeat.

With the clock in the red, Italy were awarded a penalty. Paolo Garbisi went for touch in the corner, but the flyhalf overhit his kick and the Italians fell to a sixth consecutive defeat.

Quesada's lineout coach, former Italy great Sergio Parisse, shook his head ruefully in the stands, and the two Argentine-born men looked increasingly frustrated throughout the match.

"Knowing that, after all the work we put in together during the week, we could have beaten Argentina with 30 seconds left, and that it was in our hands, hurts," Quesada told reporters.

"We had the tools and the opportunity to win, but we let it slip away. We are still missing something, we need to be more solid."
