The US State Department said the advanced PAC-3 interceptor missiles would cost $2.1bn (£1.6bn) and would be "funded entirely" by Kyiv's allies in Europe and Canada.

On Friday night Zelensky repeated his calls for the US to speed up the release of its Patriots – the only effective weapon for downing Russia's deadly ballistic missiles.

In an update on Saturday morning, Zelensky said around 200,000 households in the Dnipro region were left without electricity as well as at least 80,000 households in the Kyiv region following Russian strikes on Friday night.

He accused Russia of "deliberately targeting civilian infrastructure every day".

The US approved sale of Patriot missiles comes after a row between Zelensky and Donald Trump earlier this month when the US eased sanctions on Russia as part of a deal to secure Russian diesel.
