5:T4fd,Schools in England will be expected to set aside regular time for students to read for pleasure under changes to the national curriculum.

Primary schools will have to allocate time every day for children to read and secondary students will be given time every week to focus on entire books, poems and literature, the Department for Education (DfE) said.

Education Secretary Lucy Powell is poised to unveil the plans in Parliament on Monday, framing them as part of a larger effort to address the "ticking time bomb" of declining literacy rates.

The push to boost reading rates will also help with the "battle for attention with mindless scrolling", the DfE said.

Schools are already facing stricter guidance on phone use , that means children will not be able to access their devices at all during the school day, in a bid to reduce screen time.

The DfE statement continued: "Reading enjoyment and daily reading levels remain too low, particularly among disadvantaged children and boys."

Year 8 students will undergo a mandatory reading check to identify students who need additional help.
