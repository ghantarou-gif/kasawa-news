The ceremonial keel for the aircraft carrier USS Doris Miller will be laid next week.

CNN reported this summer that the Navy was considering naming the ship after President Trump instead. That brief controversy may have distracted from the personal courage of Doris Miller himself. And putting his name on an aircraft carrier is meant to memorialize his heroism.

Doris Miller, known to his shipmates as Dorie, was 22 years old when Japanese forces attacked the U.S. Pacific Fleet at Pearl Harbor on December 7, 1941. Miller was from a sharecropping family in Waco, Texas, hours away from any coast. But he wanted to see the world and send money to his family, so he enlisted in the Navy.

The U.S. Navy was segregated in those days, like all branches of the armed forces. Black sailors were assigned to custodial roles, in the kitchen and laundry.
