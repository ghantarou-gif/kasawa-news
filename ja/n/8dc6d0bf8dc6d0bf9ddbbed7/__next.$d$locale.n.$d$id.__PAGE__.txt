Event founder Vanessa Fullerton-Batten said she wanted to let people enjoy "music that transports you back to those heady days of the '90s club dancefloors - but in the morning".

Fullerton-Batten and her husband, Ben Kay, who DJs at the events, moved to the town earlier this year from Hertfordshire and wanted to help bring the community together.

High street coffee shop Cuppa has hosted two of the events so far where people of all ages can enjoy a mid-morning dance and a brew.

"I think there's just this sense of wanting to be connected again," said Fullerton-Batten.
