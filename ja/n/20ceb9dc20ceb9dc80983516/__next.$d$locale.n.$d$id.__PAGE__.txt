UN Women Germany is one of 13 UN Women national committees worldwide, each an independent, non-profit, non-governmental organization committed to gender equality and the empowerment of women and girls.

Their current campaign "Orange The World" runs from the start of International Day for the Elimination of Violence Against Women on November 25 to December 10, International Human Rights Day. It is designed to raise awareness around violence against women in the country and worldwide. The 2026 campaign's focus is particularly on men's responsibility in ending gender-based violence.

"In these 16 days of activism, we can deliver a lot of information. "The debate helps and it's a chance to change consciousness about the topic."

Germany captain Giulia Gwinn said in a statement via the German Football Association (DFB): "Violence against women and girls affects us all and must have no place in our society.
