The Alfa Watan sank, and its crew has not been recovered. Bulgaria conducted a search and rescue operation, which was called off on Wednesday morning.

The crew of the Able, which was damaged, was rescued by a passing Bulgarian ship. Two crew members were taken to the military hospital in Varna.
