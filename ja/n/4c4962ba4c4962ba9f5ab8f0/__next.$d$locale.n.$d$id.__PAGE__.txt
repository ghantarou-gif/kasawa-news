5:T4bb,The 2026 Youth Word of the Year, " Ragebait ," was announced on Saturday at the Frankfurt Book Fair by publisher PONS Langenscheidt.

The "Jugendwort des Jahres," or German Youth Word of the Year , is an annual campaign that documents the popularity of new slang expressions.

"Ragebait" (in English: rage bait) refers to social media content that is deliberately designed to make people angry, frustrated or morally outraged.

It aims to drive engagement, since the algorithms of platforms like Instagram, TikTok and X reward high-energy interaction. A post that generates a heated debate through angry comments, or that is widely shared due to the emotional reaction it provokes, counts as high engagement, which leads the platforms to push the content to more feeds.

"Ragebait" was on a shortlist of three words for the 2026 Youth Word of the Year, alongside "Peak" and "Crashout."

All three terms are borrowed from English, demonstrating once again the language's significant influence on German youth culture.

Around 1 million young people, aged 11 to 20, have voted since August 28 for their favorite term.

"Ragebait" obtained 36.5% of the votes, followed by 33.8% for "Peak" and 29.7% for "Crashout."d:X
