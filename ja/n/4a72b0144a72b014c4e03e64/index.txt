15:T4e8,A print by a Yorkshire artist commissioned by Nasa in the early days of space travel will go on show as part of a pop art exhibition.

Komtek I by Michael Fossick, who studied at Bradford School of Art, was created in 1962 to celebrate a Hong Kong company that produced microcomputers in the early days of space travel.

The print was part of a Nasa project to document the effort of sending the first humans to the moon and is on display as part of the Pop Art at Cartwright Hall exhibition.

The display tells the story of the British International Print Biennale, which took place at the Bradford gallery on 11 occasions between 1968 and 1990, and runs until March 2027.

While the exhibition also includes work by Andy Warhol and David Hockney, there is "definitely stuff that people won't have seen before", said curator Jill Iredale.

"It's a completely bizarre sort of connection somehow that an artist born in Sheffield that went to Bradford School of Art was somehow commissioned by Nasa," said Iredale.

"It was actually a screen print, but it was made from a very early computerised image," she said.
