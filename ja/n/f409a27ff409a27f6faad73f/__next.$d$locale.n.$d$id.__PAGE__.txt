5:T515,General Assembly President Khalilur Rahman visits the Rohingya refugee camps in Cox's Bazar.

A day after the UN’s highest court found that Myanmar had committed genocide against the Rohingya, the President of the General Assembly visited the refugee camps in Cox’s Bazar, Bangladesh, urging the world to turn the landmark judgement into action.

“Yesterday in The Hague, the highest court of the United Nations spoke. It spoke your name: Rohingya. Yes, Rohingya. Its decision was clear. What was done to you in 2017 was genocide,” Khalilur Rahman told refugees and reporters.

He called for justice for the victims, reparations for the survivors and guarantees that refugees can return home safely and with dignity .

Before becoming Bangladesh’s Foreign Minister and then Assembly President, Mr. Rahman served as his country’s top envoy on the Rohingya and visited the camps many times.

“ I did not wish to speak to you from New York. I wish to come here to the camps and stand with you at this historic moment ,” he said.
