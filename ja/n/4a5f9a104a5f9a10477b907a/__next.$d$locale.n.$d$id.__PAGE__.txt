Police officer Michèle Kiesewetter was killed by the NSU on April 25, 2007, in Heilbronn, in the German state of Baden-Württemberg .

Four and a half years after Kiesewetter's death, the identities of the people behind the murders came to light. On November 4, 2011, Beate Zschäpe, who is now imprisoned with little likelihood of release before 2035, revealed the existence of the NSU . The group also included Uwe Böhnhardt and Uwe Mundlos, who were found dead in a burned-out RV on the same day.

The two men are believed to have died in a murder-suicided following a failed bank robbery in the eastern German city of Eisenach, under circumstances that have never been fully clarified.
