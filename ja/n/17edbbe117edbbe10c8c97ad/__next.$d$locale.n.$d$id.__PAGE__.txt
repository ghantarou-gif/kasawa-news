Australian brothers Jake and Callum Robinson, aged 30 and 33, and their friend Carter Rhoad, 30, were on a surfing trip to Mexico when they were shot dead in Baja California state.

They were camping in a remote beachside area near the city of Ensenada when they were attacked during a suspected robbery. Their bodies were found days later, in a well, with gunshot wounds to their heads.

The judge on Friday ruled that the three Mexican defendants had acted with premeditation. They are yet to be sentenced for the murders.

The prosecution has asked for an "exemplary sentence" of 210 years for each of the three convicted, even though the maximum sentence in Baja California is 60 years.

Debra Robinson, mother to the Australian brothers, burst into tears when the court passed the guilty verdict.
