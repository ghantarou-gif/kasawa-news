British comedian Russell Brand has gone from a celebrity darling to a convicted rapist, after a jury on Thursday found him guilty of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault. His sentencing is scheduled for Monday.

The judge in the court case said Brand, a one-time MTV host turned recovering heroin addict and new age guru, was facing a "significant custodial term."

Aside from the guilty verdicts, Brand — who denied all charges — was also cleared of two additional counts of rape and one of attempted indecent assault.

The 51-year-old showed little emotion as the verdict was read out in the London courtroom, according to journalists in attendance. British courts don't allow cameras in.

But his wife Laura, with whom he shares three children, was said to be visibly upset. She has stood firmly behind him throughout the trial.

Many hailed the bravery of the six women who brought the charges.
