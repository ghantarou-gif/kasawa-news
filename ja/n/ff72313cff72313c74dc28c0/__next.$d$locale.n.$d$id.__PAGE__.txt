5:T42e,'Can you believe it?': SpaceX catches part of its Starship rocket on return to Earth

SpaceX has successfully caught the top part of its huge Starship rocket for the first time, after a flight lasting 22-and-a-half hours.

The rocket returned to Earth and hovered near its launch tower, whose mechanical arms - nicknamed chopsticks - then secured it.

The company had hoped to catch the bottom part of the rocket as well, but this was aborted a few minutes after the launch on Friday.

SpaceX needs to reliably recover both parts of Starship so that it can fly repeatedly and drastically reduce costs.

There were loud cheers from SpaceX staff who were watching the flight as the rocket returned to the Starbase launchpad in Boca Chica, Texas.

This is the 15th test of Starship and the first that hoped to fully recover both parts of the rocket. SpaceX wants to make the top and bottom sections of Starship fully reusable.

The 124m-tall (407ft) rocket successfully blasted off from the Starbase launchpad in Boca Chica, Texas at 16:47 local time (19:47 GMT) on Friday.d:X
