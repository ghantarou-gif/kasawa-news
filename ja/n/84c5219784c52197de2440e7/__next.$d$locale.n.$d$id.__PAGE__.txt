SINGAPORE: Indonesia’s reduction in gas exports to Singapore on Saturday (Oct 24) amounts to less than 2 per cent of the country’s total daily gas demand, said the Energy Market Authority (EMA).

In response to media queries, an EMA spokesperson said the total reduction of 31 million standard cubic feet of gas a day amounts to 17 British thermal units per day (bbtud) over a period of 18 days.

"This is less than 2 per cent of our total daily gas demand of more than 1,000 bbtud," it said, adding that it understands that importers will pursue all relevant recourse in their contracts for the gas supply disruption, said EMA.

EMA also said that it had contingency plans in place to deal with such reductions.
