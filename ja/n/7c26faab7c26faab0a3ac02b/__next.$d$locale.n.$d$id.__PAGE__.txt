UN Secretary-General António Guterres has condemned a drone attack on Kuwait on Thursday that killed two Italian nationals and injured a Kuwaiti and a Pakistani national.

In a note issued on Friday, Mr. Guterres expressed solidarity with Kuwait, extended his sincere condolences to the victims’ families and wished those injured a speedy recovery.

He said he was deeply concerned by the continued escalation in the region and called on all parties to exercise restraint and to refrain from any actions that could further undermine regional peace and security and jeopardize ongoing diplomatic efforts.

Renewed hostilities would have devastating consequences for the region and beyond, he warned, reiterating that dialogue and diplomacy remain “the only viable path towards lasting peace in the region”.

Acute food insecurity is expected to worsen over the next six months in 16 hunger hotspots worldwide, UN agencies warned on Friday .

Conflicts, violence and the intensifying El Niño weather phenomenon are driving hunger to alarming levels, according to the UN Food and Agriculture Organization ( FAO ) and the World Food Programme ( WFP ).
