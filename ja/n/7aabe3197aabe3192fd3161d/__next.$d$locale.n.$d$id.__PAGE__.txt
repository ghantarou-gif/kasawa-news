5:T4f2,After COP31: A reset for climate diplomacy?

The longest-running climate talks in history came to a disappointing conclusion on Sunday as negotiators failed to come to an agreement on a shared political direction on climate change.

After two days of extended talks, representatives from country after country, tiredness etched into each face, voiced their opposition to the so-called "Antalya Pact."

"I understand that there is no agreement on this text. For that reason, these decisions have not been adopted," said Murat Kurum, Turkish president of the COP31 climate summit .

One of the main sticking points: the words " fossil fuels ." While some nations, including petrostates like Saudi Arabia and Russia, wanted them omitted from the text, others saw it as a "step backwards" not to include them.

Around 130 parties — among them vulnerable island nations, developing countries and richer EU members — called for the reintroduction of the two words into the text, to no avail. And so many refused to support the adoption of the final COP agreement.
