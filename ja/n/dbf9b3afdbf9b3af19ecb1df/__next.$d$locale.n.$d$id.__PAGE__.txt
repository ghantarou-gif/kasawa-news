5:T513,The European Union and Indonesia signed a free trade deal on Saturday, describing it as a new chapter in economic relations between the two sides.

"This is one of the most ambitious deals the European Union has concluded in Asia," Ursula von der Leyen , president of the European Commission, said at the signing ceremony on Saturday.

"Together, we represent a market of 730 million people," she added.

The Indonesia-European Union Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA) and the additional Investment Protection Agreement (IPA) are designed to reduce trade barriers and attract investment in strategic sectors such as electric vehicles, electronics, and pharmaceuticals.

The agreement comes after almost 10 years of negotiations and is part of a wider series of trade deals by the EU .

The bloc hopes to reduce its reliance on individual economic partners as trade with both China and the US has become more difficult in recent years.

Von der Leyen also stressed the importance of international cooperation, saying that "in a time when others are closing their doors, we keep ours open."
