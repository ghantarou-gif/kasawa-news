WASHINGTON: US President Donald Trump's decision to waive sanctions and tariffs on Russia days before they were to take effect will "unfortunately" lead to additional relief for Russia and lead to further Russian aggression, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy posted on Saturday (Oct 17).

"The decision to undo all of this, to block all of it, seems wrong to me," Zelenskyy wrote on X. "It definitely helps only the Russians."

An executive order issued by Trump on Friday night waived key Russia-related US sanctions and tariffs, including duties on the country's goods and penalties for buying its oil and gas, that had been scheduled to start next week under the legislation known as the Graham Act that he signed into law last month.

Trump said the waiver was in the national interests of the United States.
