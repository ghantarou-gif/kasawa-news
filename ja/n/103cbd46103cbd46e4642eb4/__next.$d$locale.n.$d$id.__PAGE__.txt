In 2027, Macao will celebrate 10 years as a UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy – and foodie travellers are being urged to visit to sample its diverse dining scene.

Located on the western bank of China’s Pearl River Delta, the coastal special administrative region is famed for dishes influenced by Portuguese and Chinese flavours.

Combining eastern and western cultures, Macanese cuisine is considered one of the earliest examples of fusion cooking. It was designated as intangible heritage by the local government in 2012.

“2027 is a very special year,” Maria Helena de Senna Fernandes, Director at the Macao Government Tourism Office, told Euronews Travel at World Travel Market in London.

“That will be the 10th anniversary of our designation as a UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy. So definitely a lot of food events happening next year.”
