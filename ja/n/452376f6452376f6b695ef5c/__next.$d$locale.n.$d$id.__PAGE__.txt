Nov 28 : Elena Rybakina has her sights are on a maiden French Open title to complete a career Grand Slam, after becoming the first Kazakh to finish the season as world number one.

The 27-year-old enjoyed a stellar 2026 campaign, winning the Australian Open and US Open to take her Grand Slam tally to three.

"Of course, having already won three Grand Slams, I would love to win Roland Garros, though that is an extremely difficult task," Rybakina told reporters on Saturday.

"I hope to get closer to that goal next year. As for my ranking, I'll pay less attention to it moving forward. Since I've already achieved the goal of reaching the world number one spot, my main focus now is winning Grand Slams and performing consistently at all the major tournaments."
