5:T560,The Department for Work and Pensions has confirmed it was the subject of a cyber-attack, a day after benefits and pension services were taken offline.

The DWP took down several systems on Friday, with many still appearing to be offline 24 hours later. A spokesperson confirmed the attack on Saturday afternoon, adding: “Alongside the National Cyber Security Centre and Government Cyber Coordination Centre, we are working to understand the cause of an attempt to gain unauthorised access to our systems.”

The DWP makes about 2.5m regular payments a day and it is understood that reported delays are not related to the cyber incident. The department urged people to be alert to any scams related to the outage and stressed that the government would never ask people to confirm bank details or click on a link.

DWP insiders said payments for Friday would already have been sent earlier in the week, but there could be a backlog of manual payments running into tens of thousands due early next week to be processed over the weekend. Internal DWP systems were restored on Friday, allowing officials to begin working again, but the public-facing portal remained offline.
