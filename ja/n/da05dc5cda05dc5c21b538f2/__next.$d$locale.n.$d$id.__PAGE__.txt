MILAN: Italy's data protection agency said on Saturday (Nov 14) it had fined Meta Platforms €805 million (US$887 million) after finding the company had accessed Android users' photographs and videos without obtaining their consent or informing them.

Meta had access to Android users' photos and videos through a Facebook app feature that suggested content from users' personal galleries that they could post, the authority said in a statement.

Meta has 60 days to disable the feature by default and seek users' consent separately, it said.

A Meta spokesperson said the company would appeal, strongly disagreeing with the authority's decision and calling the fine "disproportionate".
