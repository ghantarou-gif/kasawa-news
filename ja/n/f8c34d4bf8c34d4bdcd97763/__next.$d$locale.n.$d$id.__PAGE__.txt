But scrum-half Seren Lockwood's 35th-minute snipe gave the game a much-needed spark and the visitors a 7-0 advantage at the break.

Wales lost centre Freya Bell to a serious-looking knee injury, but the impact of the bench proved the difference as Kelsey Jones drove over off lineout ball, before Lockwood finished a rare moment of class at the death.

It was far from an impressive performance from Sean Lynn's resurgent Wales, but given their previous results, his side have now proven the ability to win ugly.

The two sides will meet again in Kumagaya City on Saturday, 31 October.

The hosts, who were playing in a double header with the men's side to celebrate Japanese rugby's centenary, had twice run Ireland close before their meeting with Wales.
