Sen. Ron Johnson opposes tying government funding to Iran war "We're not going to shut down the war," Sen. Ron Johnson said as Republicans and Democrats struggle to reach a government funding deal.

Sen. Ron Johnson on efforts to avert government shutdown during lame duck session

Sen. Ron Johnson, R-Wis., speaks to reporters at the U.S. Capitol in Washington on Nov. 16.

Republican Sen. Ron Johnson of Wisconsin, who has defended President Trump's war with Iran, said Friday that he opposes tying government funding to an end to the conflict as Congress struggles to avert a shutdown before Christmas.

"We're not going to shut down the war. That is President Trump's power. He's exercising his constitutional authority," Johnson, who chairs the Senate Budget Committee, told Morning Edition.

Congress faces a Dec. 11 deadline to keep federal agencies operating, giving Democrats leverage in the Republican-controlled Senate, where most legislation requires 60 votes to advance. But both parties face divisions. Democrats disagree over whether to use the funding deadline to force an end to the war, while Republicans are weighing bipartisan negotiations against a party-line approach to securing additional military spending.d:X
