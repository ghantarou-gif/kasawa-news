The German president plans to spend several days touring China before concluding his visit in Beijing, where he will meet with China's leaders.

His visit comes just as the Chinese Communist Party's Central Committee is convening for its plenary session, a major annual event that foreign dignitaries usually avoid when timing their visits.

The focus of the president's five-day trip will be "Germany's diverse and complex relations with China, as well as the challenges involved," his office said in a statement. In addition to sites of cultural exchange, the itinerary will also include a visit to a Volkswagen plant in Hefei.

The automotive industry, in particular, illustrates the dilemma facing Germany's China policy. China is one of the most important markets for German automotive and machinery manufacturers, as well as for the chemical industry. At the same time, state-supported overcapacity in China is putting pressure on European manufacturers.
