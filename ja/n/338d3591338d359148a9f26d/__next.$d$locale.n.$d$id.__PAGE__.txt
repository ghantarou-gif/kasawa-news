EU spokesperson Christian Wigand made no mention of the US or external pressure on Thursday but also did not repudiate the reports.

"We were not comfortable with the framing of some of the discussions at the event," he told reporters in Brussels.

"The organizers chose to frame some of the discussions in way which does not align with the official positions held by the EU," he said.
