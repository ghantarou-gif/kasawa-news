5:T522,Reigning world marathon champion Peres Jepchirchir has been banned for three years for a doping violation.

The 33-year-old, who also won Olympic gold in 2021, was provisionally suspended by the Athletics Integrity Unit (AIU) in October after an out-of-competition sample tested positive for erythropoietin (EPO).

The sample was collected in Kenya prior to Jepchirchir winning the Sydney Marathon on 30 August 2026 and returned an adverse finding on 17 September.

She is one of three high-profile Kenyan athletes to have been sanctioned. Veronica Loleo has also been banned for three years after admitting using EPO, while Ronald Korir has received a six-year ban after failing to explain abnormalities found in his biological passport.

EPO is a natural substance produced within the kidneys that stimulates the creation of new red blood cells.

If injected, it increases the amount of red blood cells, which carry oxygen to the muscles, in the blood stream but is only detectable in urine or blood for a short window of time.

Jepchirchir's other major wins came at the New York Marathon in 2021, the Boston Marathon in 2022 and the London Marathon in 2024.

While she denied using EPO, she signed an admission of violations form and accepted disqualification of all her competitive results since 17 July 2026.d:X
