An airline pilot was among three Saudi citizens killed in attacks targeting Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport, the kingdom's civil aviation authority said on Friday.

The first attack on Thursday targeted airport facilities, while the second struck a Saudi aircraft, the Saudi statement said. It did not identify those responsible, though the Houthis claimed to have targeted the airport with a ballistic missile.

Several people "of different nationalities" were wounded in the attacks, the kingdom said.
