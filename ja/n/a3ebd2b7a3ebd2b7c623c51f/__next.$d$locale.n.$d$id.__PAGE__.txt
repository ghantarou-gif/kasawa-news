Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO of the world's largest chipmaker on Monday, sending the company's stock soaring as President Trump claimed credit for brokering his close ally's return.

It was a stunning reversal of fortune for Huang. He resigned less than two weeks ago, following news reports of extramarital affairs, including with a woman U.S. intelligence agencies suspected of being a Chinese spy.

In a statement put out just before the market opened, Nvidia said its board voted unanimously in favor of bringing back Huang after a series of conversations and meetings, as well as input from people at "senior levels" of the U.S. government.
