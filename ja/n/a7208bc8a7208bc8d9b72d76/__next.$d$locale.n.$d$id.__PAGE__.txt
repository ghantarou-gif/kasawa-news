5:T54c,AI harming students? German teachers warn of tech risks

Jakob is 15 years old and attends a secondary school for more academic students, known in Germany as a gymnasium. He and his classmates have already been using artificial intelligence-enabled tools, or AI , for the last two and a half years.

"I had a French test today, and yesterday I had ChatGPT explain a lot of the topics to me again," the student told DW. Jakob isn't his real name, because he doesn't want to publicly admit he and many others of his age use AI to do their homework.

Math, German, English, chemistry and even physics: In one click, the solution or the path to a solution is available thanks to AI.

In science, this is called "cognitive outsourcing." Basically, the required thinking is shifted to another device, whether that's a calculator, notepad or a large language model (LLM) like ChatGPT .

There's a difference with AI, though. A notepad or calculator can take over a step or two but rarely the whole thought process. That's not the same for an LLM, which can handle the entire problem-solving process.
