5:T491,Dungeons and Dragons is a screen time alternative for kids Some parents, fed up with screens, have found an analog oasis in the role-playing game invented in the 1970s.

First published in 1974, Dungeons & Dragons is now being embraced by a new generation looking for a screen-free hobby.

Dungeons & Dragons has captivated players for more than 50 years.

In its early days, some parents complained that the role-playing game kept kids holed up inside for hours at a time. NPR even did a 10-minute piece on it in 1979.

More recently, D&D has come back into popular culture through shows including Stranger Things , a 2023 blockbuster movie and an influx of interest during the COVID-19 lockdowns .

Now, what was once a nerdy pastime has become a screen-free parent's dream — a game that relies on imagination, a role of the dice and in-person communication.
