NEW DELHI: At least 11 people have died and more than 180 have been hospitalised after drinking suspected tainted alcohol in India's northeastern state of Manipur.

The victims fell ill after consuming the liquor, Chief Minister Yumnam Khemchand Singh said in a statement on Friday (Nov 20).

"Over 180 patients have been admitted in the two hospitals so far, while the death toll has reached 11 in the last 48 hours," the statement said.

He also ordered an enquiry into the incident, saying those responsible would be arrested and punished.
