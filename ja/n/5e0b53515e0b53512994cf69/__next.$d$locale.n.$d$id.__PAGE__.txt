Helsinki has become the latest European city to vote in favour of banning advertising for fossil fuels and other carbon-intensive products and services.

The landmark proposal was brought to the City Council of Helsinki by Councillor Isaac Jyväsjärvi and 27 fellow councillors. It follows previous attempts to introduce restrictions on outdoor advertising from “carbon-intensive industries” that gained momentum in 2021 but failed to reach a majority support.

The exact scope of the restriction is to be determined ahead of the next advertising contract period, but campaigners say it could one day rid the streets of billboards promoting industries such as aviation, shipping, tourism and construction – as well as fossil fuels.

Existing advertising contracts in the city run until 2035, meaning it may take almost a decade for the ban to come into effect.
