5:T50c,VW logs €5.86 billion Q3 operating loss on Porsche writedown

The Volkswagen Group on Thursday posted its worst quarterly numbers in more than a decade.

The company reported an operating loss of €5.86 billion (roughly $6.53 billion) during July, August and September. One-off costs, not least a massive writedown in its stake in struggling sister company Porsche , explained the vast bulk of the losses.

The world's second-largest carmaker is battling mounting competition from and falling demand in the core Chinese market.

It is pursuing a major restructuring and downsizing program , trying to bolster falling profit margins, and executives said the difficulties highlighted the urgency of this process.

"The nine-month results make one thing clear: We cannot afford to lose any time. We must act quickly and decisively," chief financial officer Arno Antlitz said in a video statement.

What were the main takeaways from VW's Q3 figures?
