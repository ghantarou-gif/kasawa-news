5:T4ca,F1 in Rwanda: Sportswashing or African opportunity?

Rwanda will welcome back F1 to the African continent from 2030, but what does the move mean? Image: AP Photo/picture alliance

Rwanda's sports portfolio has grown after confirming it will host Formula One races from the early 2030s . The track is to be built in Bugesera near Kigali International Airport, which Qatar Airways, an F1 sponsor, owns 60% of.

"To be an African and to see it happening in real time, for me in my lifetime because Kyalami [the track in South Africa where F1 was last hosted in Africa] was before I was born, I'm more than happy to have it on the continent," South African presenter and content creator Nqobile Khwezi told Sky Sports.

"To be able to give that taste back to the rest of the world and the audience of Formula One would be an absolute delight."

F1 have also highlighted the benefits for the host nation.

"Through this race we have the opportunity to inspire and bring the passion of F1 to our more than 100 million fans across Africa and firmly believe that sport can be a positive force for good," an F1 spokesperson told The Athletic.
