Across 245 pages the inquiry looks at everything from border controls to how well contact tracing worked.

The report said the virus was allowed to spread virtually unchecked in the first few months after community testing and contact tracing was abandoned in mid-March 2020, costing lives.

Ministers said this was because testing capacity was being overwhelmed.

NHS Test and Trace was launched two months later and eventually became one of the biggest testing services in the world per head of population.

In the end, more than £30bn was spent across the UK during the pandemic on testing, tracing and isolating.

But in those early months the four UK nations were "effectively blind" to how quickly Covid was spreading, the inquiry said.
