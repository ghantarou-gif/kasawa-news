US First Lady Melania Trump has secured an agreement between Russia and Ukraine to temporarily halt military operations in the Russian-occupied city of Oleshky, the White House announced on Friday. The intent is to allow humanitarian aid deliveries and evacuate civilians trapped near the front line.

The localised ceasefire is scheduled to begin on 29 October, with both sides agreeing to suspend military action in the southern Ukrainian city and establish a designated safe passage for residents wishing to leave, according to the White House.

Oleshky lies on the Russian-occupied eastern bank of the Dnipro River, opposite the Ukrainian-controlled city of Kherson. Before Russia's full-scale invasion, it had approximately 24,000 residents. According to White House estimates, only 1,700 to 2,000 remain, including around 50 children.
