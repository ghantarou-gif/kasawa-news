During the party conference in the eastern city of Dresden, a majority of the approximately 600 delegates voted against a motion put forth by several founding BSW members calling for a clearer distinction from the far right.

The basis for the vote came after the AfD came out on top in September's state election in the eastern state of Saxony-Anhalt with 39 seats. With the BSW's five seats, the two parties on the opposite extremes of the political spectrum would combine for a majority of the 83 seats.

BSW Party founder Sahra Wagenknecht and party leader Fabio De Masi have advocated for the-called "Magdeburg Model," named after the capital of Saxony-Anhalt.

This approach rejects a formal coalition with the AfD, offers no support for AfD candidate in the election of a state premiere, and calls for no official tolerance for far-fight platforms.
