5:T56e,What is Nasa’s Artemis III mission and will it go to the Moon? - BBC News

Nasa has announced more details about its next Artemis mission, which is set to launch in the summer of 2027.

The Artemis III mission will not head to the Moon. Instead four astronauts will fly to low Earth orbit where they will dock with two prototype lunar landers, one made by Jeff Bezos' Blue Origin company and the other by Elon Musk's SpaceX.

This will pave the way for the Artemis IV mission, currently scheduled for 2028, which will see astronauts land on the lunar surface for the first time since 1972.

Artemis III has been described by Nasa as the most complicated mission it's ever attempted.

It involves four astronauts, three rocket launches, three different spacecraft and two dockings. It will last for 14 days, although it could be extended to 17 days if needed.

The first rocket to launch will be Blue Origin's New Glenn rocket, blasting off from Florida. It will be carrying its prototype lander called Blue Moon. No astronauts will be on board and the lander will be placed into low Earth orbit.
