5:T503,Democrats gather in Denver to chart path ahead Democrats from across the country descended on Denver for a two-day conference to chart the course of their party after sweeping victories in the midterm elections.

Texas Senator-elect James Talarico speaks at the Mission Ballroom during the What's Next Denver political convention on Nov. 12, 2026.

DENVER — Democrats descended on Denver for a two-day conference that was half victory party, half sober look at the road ahead on delivering for the voters who elected them.

The "What's Next" conference, hosted by the group Majority Democrats, was scheduled long before Democrats outperformed expectations with their best electoral performance since Watergate. It brought together rising stars — like incoming Gov. Rob Sand in Iowa and Sen.-elect James Talarico in Texas — along with strategists, organizers, state and federal lawmakers and potential presidential contenders.

Held at a concert hall, with colorful artwork of party heavy hitters dotting the venue, Democrats attended sessions like "What Voters Want" and "What Needs To Change." The overwhelming emphasis from speakers was that Democrats have to earn back trust.
