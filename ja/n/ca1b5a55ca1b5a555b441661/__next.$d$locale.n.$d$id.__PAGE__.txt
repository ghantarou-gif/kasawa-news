5:T445,A father whose two-year-old boy fell from a fourth floor window of a Cyprus hotel has been fined €3,000 (£2,500) for causing his death.

Joshua Foster thought the window was closed when he placed his son on a sill to show him the view of a waterpark and "lost control" of him , Paphos District Court previously heard.

The toddler fell nearly 40ft (12.1m) at the five-star King Evelthon Beach Hotel and Resort on 12 July, the first day of the family's holiday in Chlorakas.

Foster, 37, from Essex, admitted causing death through a reckless, rash or dangerous act and was sentenced earlier.

He told reporters afterwards: "Thank you for the support. I'm looking forward to going home and being with my wife and daughter."

Later, the family said in a statement: "We are relieved that this process is now over, but for our family, the consequences of this tragedy will never end.

"Our son was deeply loved and will be forever missed. His loss is something we carry with us every day."
