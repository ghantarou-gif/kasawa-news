Police said the pilot of the single-engine aircraft crashed near the Greenside private airfield, off Brand Highway in Muchea, north-east of Perth, this morning.

Emergency services responded to the incident, during which the aircraft sustained "significant damage", police said.

A man in his 60s and another man in his 30s were treated at the scene.

Department of Fire and Emergency Services (DFES) district officer Gareth Deeg attended the scene and said one occupant extracted himself from the aircraft, while the other person was assisted out.
