More than 300,000 people displaced since early March remain internally displaced in Lebanon, where the humanitarian crisis is “far from over,” according to the UN’s aid coordination office ( OCHA ).

Despite civilians’ willingness to return home, continued Israeli military activity and presence in the south are making it very difficult, UN Spokesperson Stéphane Dujarric told journalists on Friday.

This week alone, further demolitions, fires and strikes by Israeli forces were reported across Southern Lebanon, both within the so-called combat zone declared by Israel – from which residents were ordered to evacuate – and beyond it.

Although close to 970,000 people have returned to their communities in the south, their situation is often dire: homes have been damaged, while essential services are disrupted and making a living has become ever more challenging.

In Nabatieh Governorate, for example, insecurity and restrictions on access to agricultural land are disrupting the olive harvest there.
