24:T107a,The Israeli military crossed into Hamas-controlled central Gaza on Wednesday in its first ground operation across the “yellow line” boundary since a fragile ceasefire was agreed last October, forcing dozens of displaced Palestinians to flee their tents.

Israeli tanks rolled along the Salah al-Din road , Gaza’s main north-south route, a day after the US-backed Board of Peace, a body overseeing the ceasefire, accused Israel and Hamas of failing to honour their commitments to end hostilities.

An Israeli military spokesperson said troops were carrying out an operation targeting “Hamas infrastructure” west of the yellow line in central Gaza .

The operation lasted about 24 hours and on Thursday the military said it had been “completed”.

The IDF ​has not commented extensively on the operation, which came as the Israeli opposition politician, Gadi Eisenkot, has been seeking to form a government without the caretaker prime minister, Benjamin Netanyahu, who has built his political reputation on promises to keep Israel safe.

A Gaza security source told AFP there was “intense Israeli artillery shelling” north of Nuseirat refugee camp and reported Israeli military vehicles in central Gaza “amid ongoing gunfire and airstrikes since midday”.

Hamas condemned the Israeli raid and the forced displacement of Palestinians as “a flagrant violation of the ceasefire agreement” and a dangerous escalation. The Israeli military did not immediately respond to Hamas’s statement.

Residents reported hearing tank fire in the area. Palestinian medics said one person was killed in an Israeli airstrike near the site of the raid.

After the statement from the IDF that it had concluded the tank raid to dismantle a suspected Hamas tunnel, the defence ministry’s body in charge of civilian affairs in Palestinian territories, Cogat, said Palestinians would again be free to move in the targeted areas near al-Bureij and Nuseirat refugee camps.

A map of the area around the Nuseirat refugee camp in the Gaza Strip.

In separate incidents on Wednesday, Palestinian medics said two people were killed by Israeli fire, one in Deir al-Balah and the other in Gaza City. The Israeli military said it was unaware of any incident in Deir al-Balah and had struck a militant in Gaza City.

Israel has stepped up its attacks on Hamas, which it says has been regrouping and rearming. Hamas has accused Israel of trying to derail the US-brokered ceasefire agreed in October 2025.

Israeli strikes have continued since the ceasefire, and Hamas remains armed.

Last week, an Israeli airstrike on Nasser hospital in southern Gaza killed six people, including a surgeon and a disabled man, and wounded 28 others, according to the hospital director.

The body overseeing Donald Trump’s plan to end the war in Gaza has accused Israel and Hamas of failing to honour their commitments to end hostilities, leaving Palestinians in dire conditions and the ceasefire at risk of collapse.

In its second report to the UN security council since the truce began 13 months ago, the Trump-led Board of Peace said the gap between what was agreed and what was happening on the ground remained the biggest threat to the deal.
