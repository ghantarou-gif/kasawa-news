New York City is celebrating the 100th edition of Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade on Saturday, two days later than planned after a powerful storm forced organisers to postpone one of the United States’ best-known holiday traditions.

Originally scheduled for Thanksgiving Day on Thursday, 26 November, the event was rescheduled following consultations between Macy’s, city officials and the New York Police Department (NYPD) over safety concerns.

Spirit of America performers dance along Central Park West during the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York City, Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026.

"The safety of our participants and spectators is our top priority," Macy’s said in a statement on Wednesday.

Despite the disruption, Saturday’s programme is expected to feature 100 balloons, elaborate floats, marching bands and around 1,000 clowns.

The postponement presented a major logistical challenge for organisers, who had spent more than two years preparing for the milestone event.
