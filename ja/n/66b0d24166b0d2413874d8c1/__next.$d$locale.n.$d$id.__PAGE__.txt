Sally Rooney’s books will no longer be sold in U.K. after support for pro-Palestinian group The Irish author said her books can no longer be sold in the UK after she pledged the royalties to a banned pro-Palestinian group.

Sally Rooney's books are displayed for sale at a bookshop in London, Thursday, Oct. 15, 2026.

Irish author Sally Rooney has long been described as "the voice of a generation" for her best-selling novels that are ostensibly about millennial relationships but also explore deeper questions of class, gender and politics.

Now the 35-year-old writer's books will no longer be sold in the UK because of her own closely-held political beliefs. She had promised to donate the revenues to Palestine Action, a pro-Palestinian group that the UK has designated a terrorist organization.

"Current legislation in the UK has led to circumstances where Faber cannot pass on UK earnings to Sally Rooney for her work. To sell a writer's work and not be able to pass on earnings is an unconscionable situation for a publisher."
