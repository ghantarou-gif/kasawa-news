5:T40c,Consultants and specialist doctors have begun a 24-hour strike as part of an ongoing dispute over pay.

It follows a separate 24-hour strike on Friday in which resident doctors took industrial action as part of the same dispute.

The British Medical Association (BMA) rejected a recommended 3.5% pay rise and wants to restore the value of doctors' pay to 2008 levels.

Health Minister Robbie Butler said he had accepted the recommended rise but could not implement it without sufficient funding for his department.

Niall Corrigan, a consultant psychiatrist in the Belfast Trust, was among those on the picket line outside the Ulster Hospital.

He said they were on strike "to advocate for the health service", which he said had been "under-resourced" in recent years.

He said he sympathised with patients who have appointments during the strike but if they did not take action then there would be "continuing degradation of our health service".
