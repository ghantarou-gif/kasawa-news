North Melbourne has defeated Richmond to secure the AFLW minor premiership.

The Kangaroos are the first club in the AFLW to win the minor premiership in three consecutive seasons.

St Kilda ended its season with a loss to Port Adelaide, the club's 11th defeat this season.

Jasmine Garner has spearheaded North Melbourne to a third consecutive AFLW minor premiership in a rousing 68-point victory over Richmond.

Knowing a win would make the Kangaroos the first team to top the ladder three straight times, the captain sparkled in her team's 15.11 (101) to 5.3 (33) thumping at Windy Hill on Saturday.

Her 28 disposals and one goal contribution firmed her position for the league's best and fairest award, the star midfielder playing an integral role in helping North finish its campaign 11-1.
