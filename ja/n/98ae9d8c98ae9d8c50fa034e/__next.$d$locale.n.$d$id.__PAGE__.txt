Both are coming off chastening defeats last weekend - the Wallabies taken apart by England at Twickenham, and Scotland taken to the cleaners by New Zealand at Murrayfield.

This is a fixture that stokes the fires of Scotland's Australia-born captain Sione Tuipulotu, but making amends for that second-half no-show against the All Blacks is providing the primary motivation.

"It is always hard to watch the footage back after a defeat like that where we missed some big opportunities and let ourselves down in certain areas," Tuipulotu said.

"As a group, we have confronted that and I've been very proud of the boys in terms of how they have rallied this week.

"We know that Australia will also be a wounded team coming here after what happened against England at Twickenham.
