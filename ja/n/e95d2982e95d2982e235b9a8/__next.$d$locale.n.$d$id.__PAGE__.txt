SAN FRANCISCO: A shooting at a Halloween store in northern California killed one person and wounded six on Friday (Oct 30), police said, adding investigators had not yet identified who was responsible.

The incident occurred shortly before 8.30pm at a Spirit Halloween store at a shopping mall in Fairfield, about 76 kilometres northeast of San Francisco.

Fairfield Police Chief Dan Marshall told reporters one person was killed and six others were hospitalised with gunshot wounds. At least three were in critical condition, he added.

"There is still a very active crime scene and investigation going on right now," Marshall said late Friday, noting police had not yet identified the "suspect or suspects" responsible.
