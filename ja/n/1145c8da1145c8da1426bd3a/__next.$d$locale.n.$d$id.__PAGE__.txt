People look at a pile of schoolbags, books and shoes in a classroom after an explosion at a middle school on the outskirts of Asadabad, the capital of Afghanistan's eastern Kunar province, on Saturday.

KABUL, Afghanistan — An explosion at a primary and middle school in the eastern Afghan province of Kunar Saturday killed 10 people and wounded another 110, most of them children, officials said, adding that the blast appeared to have originated in a neighboring depot for explosives.

Kunar Culture and Information Director Sami ul Haq Bayan said initial information indicated the blast at the school on the outskirts of the provincial capital, Asadabad, occurred due to negligence at the depot storing explosives belonging to mining companies. An investigation into the incident was underway, he said.

Interior ministry spokesman Abdul Mateen Qani put the death toll at 10, and said 110 had been wounded.

Footage from a ward in the hospital showed young children, several with bandaged heads and some lying two to a bed.
