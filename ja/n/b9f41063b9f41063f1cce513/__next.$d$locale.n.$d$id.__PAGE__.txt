There is a clamour among some for Manchester City to be stripped of their silverware and also be relegated after they were found guilty of multiple Premier League financial rule breaches .

The 45-year-old was on the pitch at Sunderland when he learned City had won their first Premier League title in 2012 thanks to Sergio Aguero's legendary injury time match winner.

He was still part of Jose Mourinho's squad when United finished runners-up to Pep Guardiola's City side in 2018 and was part of Ole Gunnar Solskjaer's coaching staff when it happened again in 2020-21.

However, Carrick says getting a title winners' medal years after the event would not make up for missing out at the time.

"In football, the strive to win, the emotion and the feeling about winning, a lot of it is in the moment," he said.

"You can't take that back. It's the moment you strive for. That's essentially what we're all in it for. That's the feeling we all crave.
