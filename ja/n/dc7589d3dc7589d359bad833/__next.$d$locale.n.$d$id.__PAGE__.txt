Four years later, he is working as a butcher at Metzgerei Lederer in southern Germany , having completed a three-year apprenticeship at the butcher shop.

He is not alone as the number of Indian workers subject to social security contributions in Germany has grown sevenfold over the past decade, to over 140,000 professionals.
