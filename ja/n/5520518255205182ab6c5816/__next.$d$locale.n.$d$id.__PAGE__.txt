5:T432,A car bomb has exploded in the central Colombian city of Villavicencio, injuring at least 23 people.

The explosive device was detonated in front of the offices of the regional government, in the city centre, damaging several buildings.

No group has so far admitted carrying out the attack - the latest in a series using explosives to target official buildings and security forces' headquarters.

Colombian President Abelardo de la Espriella blamed "narcoterrorism", but gave no details. Armed criminal groups have recently stepped up attacks in what appears to be retaliation for an offensive ordered against them by De la Espriella.

Writing on social media, the president said that his government would "bluntly and forcefully" respond , external to "every drop of Colombian blood spilled".

"There will be no impunity and no retreat," he added.

Three weeks ago, three adults and a baby were killed in a car bomb attack near army barracks in Tolima province .
