All aboard survived after the crash prompted a massive rescue operation for passengers and crew who plunged into the Mississippi River as the cruise boat took on water and began to topple over.

Video from the scene showed the much larger Atlantic Asphalt tanker hit the cruise boat while it was docked as metal and glass crashed and crunched.
