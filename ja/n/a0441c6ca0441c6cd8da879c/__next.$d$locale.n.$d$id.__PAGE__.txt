Speaking to reporters in New York ahead of a visit to Egypt, António Guterres addressed growing public discontent in Europe and beyond, as people have taken to the streets to express frustration over the rising cost of living.

“Everywhere, pressures are building over the price of food, fuel and other basics,” he said . “ Interest rates are up – for people and countries. And for young people, the promise of a better future is slipping further out of reach.”

While Mr. Guterres clearly denounced the violence and destruction seen during days of protests in France, Italy and elsewhere – stressing that those responsible for criminal activities must be held accountable – he said legitimate grievances of the protestors cannot be overlooked.

Protestors demonstrate by the Arc de Triomphe in Paris, France.

“ We must connect the dots and better understand how deeply linked those very real and peacefully expressed grievances are to the wider turmoil in our world ,” he told journalists.
