5:T49e,The untold stories of children born from the Windrush generation have become the centrepiece of a documentary.

First Born Second: Voices of the Children of the Windrush Era focuses on seven Bristol voices on what it meant to grow up black and British in the 1970s and 1980s.

Film co-producer and project curator Roger Griffith MBE said he wanted to champion those pioneering parents, while sharing how their children grew up in the "rich tapestry" of a Bristolian, British, black experience while overcoming "racism, institutional discrimination and a clash of cultures".

Broadcaster Sherrie Eugene-Hart shares her story and said she hoped people were left with a sense of pride, hope and achievement.

The Windrush generation was invited to come from the Caribbean to the UK to help rebuild post-war Britain.

Several hundred people who migrated were Jamaican, but others arrived from islands including Trinidad, St Lucia, Grenada and Barbados - many of whom travelled on the HMT Empire Windrush.

The boat has since became a symbol of a wider mass-migration movement.
