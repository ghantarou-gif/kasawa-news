A scientific study carried out by the University of Copenhagen has found that drinking coffee regularly may affect the functioning of the digestive system, especially bowel movements and colon contractions and is also linked to greater diversity of gut bacteria.

The study was published in the journal "Gut Microbiology", which specialises in research on gut microorganisms, and aimed to explore the relationship between habitual coffee consumption and the physiological conditions inside the digestive system.

What does coffee do inside your digestive system?

The research team used a small electronic capsule that participants swallowed to track the changes that occurred as it travelled through the digestive system.

The capsule measured a set of indicators, including the time it took contents to pass through the gastrointestinal tract, temperature, acidity levels and intestinal contractions.

The study involved 61 healthy adults in Denmark, whom the researchers divided into two groups according to how much coffee they consumed.
