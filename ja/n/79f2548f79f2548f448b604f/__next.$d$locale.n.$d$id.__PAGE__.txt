At least 17 people, including 13 women and a child, were killed in a stampede at the Durga Puja religious festival in North Kolkata that was likely caused by sudden rainfall.

"Today (17/10/26), at around 19:40 hrs, a stampede occurred at the exit gate of the Sarada Park (Chor Bagan) Puja Pandal in the Girish Park Police Station area due to heavy crowds of pandal visitors amid rain," the Kolkata Police said in a statement, adding that the injured were taken to the hospital.

West Bengal's Chief Minister Suvendhu Adhikari told reporters that the fatalities included a seven-year-old girl and that 25 others were injured in a stampede.

Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi called the incident "extremely distressing" and announced interim compensation for the families of those who died.

The largest annual Hindu festival in India's eastern state of West Bengal, Durga Puja attracts thousands of devotees to temporary structures called pandals that house idols of the goddess Durga.
