Sitting at a desk all day hurts. 5 solutions to relieve aches and pains Sitting at a desk all day hurts. And it isn't good for us. Strategies to make it a little more comfortable, from eye muscle exercises to alleviate eye strain to a back pain-fighting body movement.

Back pain, eye strain, sluggishness, shoulder aches, restlessness.

If you sit in front of a computer for long periods of time, whether it's for work or leisure, you've probably experienced one of these symptoms.

But there are ways to relieve some of this discomfort — and even combat some of the health risks that may come with prolonged sitting. That includes walking breaks, ergonomic solutions and quick, simple stretches.

Here's a roundup of tips that have been previously featured on Life Kit.

We know that a sedentary lifestyle isn't great for human health. Studies have shown it can lead to fatigue, weight gain and back and joint pain — and it's been linked to chronic diseases like obesity, heart disease and cancer.
