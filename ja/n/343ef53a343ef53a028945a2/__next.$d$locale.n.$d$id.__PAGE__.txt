Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus met the band ’s six members – Jungwon, Jay, Jake, Sunghoon, Sunoo and Ni-ki – in Korea to celebrate their work with WHO for World Blood Donor Day in June.

The campaign harnessed music, social media and the band’s global fan community, known as ENGENE, to encourage voluntary, unpaid blood donation.

“ Blood is the most precious gift that anyone can give to another person – the gift of life ,” WHO chief Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus said on Friday.

“I am proud of all ENHYPEN members’ commitment and collaboration with WHO to promote blood donation and save lives,” said Tedros.

“ The last campaign was a great success, reaching millions of people. ENHYPEN and ENGENE have the real power to influence the world .”
