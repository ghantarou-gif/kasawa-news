Backlash as American tech giants look to build data centers in Australia

一昨日

Facing legal challenges and public backlash at home, American tech giants are looking abroad for new opportunities in Australia. But the reception down under may not be quite as warm as they hope.

Facing legal challenges and public backlash at home, American tech giants have been looking abroad for new opportunities, and Australia is high on their list. But as Kristina Kukolja reports from Melbourne, the reception down under may not be quite as warm as they would hope.

KRISTINA KUKOLJA, BYLINE: The sound of native birds as they fly over fields as far as the eye can see, these were once Australia's largest goldfields. Now, the vast farmland outside Melbourne is the setting for a medium-sized data center being proposed by Australian developer RV Energy.

ALAN FORD: We know nothing. No one has told us anything to do with this data center. We've heard it by hearsay in the media.

KUKOLJA: That's Alan Ford, a fifth-generation farmer whose land borders the planned site, an abandoned quarry that also faces a residential area.

FORD: For us, it's going to be a real eyesore to start with, but the biggest problem is going to be noise and the energy. Where's this energy going to come from? We're talking about a massive amount of energy.
