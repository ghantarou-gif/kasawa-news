According to a recently published joint statement , $2.9 billion (€2.62bn) is needed for the United Nation's Humanitarian Needs and Response Plan. This funding is intended to provide life-saving aid to 20.4 million people in Sudan. So far, however, only 47% of the required funds have been raised.

A total of 33.7 million people in the war- and famine-stricken country are dependent on humanitarian aid. Some 15.4 million alone require emergency shelter and basic household items. However, due to a lack of funding, aid organizations have been able to assist only 10%.

More than three and a half years after the war began between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF), Sudan is still the site of the world's largest humanitarian and displacement crisis . About 8.6 million people fled within the country to escape the escalating violence and more than 4.6 million fled to neighboring countries. Another 5.2 million have returned to their regions of origin despite the ongoing war. However, their towns and infrastructure have been destroyed.
