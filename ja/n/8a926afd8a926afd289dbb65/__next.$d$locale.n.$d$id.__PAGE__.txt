French unions and student groups are set to take to the streets for another day of protests on Saturday, while the Yellow Vests movement plans a separate rally in central Paris despite a ban by authorities. The demonstrations follow weeks of youth-led unrest and nationwide strikes that drew hundreds of thousands of workers protesting over the rising cost of living. Follow our liveblog for the latest updates.

New protests across France are planned for Saturday — the fourth consecutive weekend — with Paris as the focal point, where the Yellow Vests intend to maintain a demonstration in the city centre despite a ban by authorities.

It will be a day under tight security, just two days after a large-scale national strike that saw between 575,000 and 1.6 million people protest.

Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez said 70,000 police officers and security forces would be deployed across the country.

"If there are any rioters or looters ... we'll be ready and waiting for them," he said.
