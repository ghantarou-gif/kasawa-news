5:T5a3,Two years of fan pressure and criticism has forced Borussia Dortmund to drop a sponsorship deal with Germany's largest arms manufacturer, Rheinmetall.

The Bundesliga club will not renew its deal with Rheinmetall beyond its expiry in June 2027, at the end of this season. Dortmund chairman Carsten Cramer confirmed the news on the club's website ahead of its AGM on Sunday, which may have proved a potential flashpoint. He acknowledged that "our partnership has been controversial from the outset" but lamented the loss of income.

"It reduces our financial room for manoeuvre. We will have to reprioritize planned investments and carefully examine how we can find viable solutions."

Though he welcomed the news, one of the leaders of the fan revolt against the partnership, Michael Schulze von Glaser, warned this is likely not the end of the road.

"I think in the end it was a victory for the fans. But I'm also a bit skeptical about what the future will bring us," the Dortmund fan and former head of the German Peace Society told DW. "Football fans in Germany always must be skeptical and always must watch what the club is doing.”
