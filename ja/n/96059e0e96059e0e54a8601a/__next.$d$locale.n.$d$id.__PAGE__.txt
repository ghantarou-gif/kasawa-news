The 29-year-old top seed exited in the last four on Saturday as Spaniard Alejandro Davidovich Fokina pulled off a surprise 5-7 6-4 6-3 win, only his second in eight meetings with Zverev.

Germany's Zverev, who is guaranteed to end the season as world number one, has had little opportunity to rest between the US Open, Laver Cup, Beijing Masters and Shanghai.

"I feel like I've actually, in a way, maybe overachieved this week because of how much tennis I played," Zverev said.

"I carried a little bit of injury, I carried fatigue into the tournament, and I'm OK to lose to a player like this today."

Repeatedly exposed on the forehand side, Zverev surrendered his first break of the match to an exquisite backhand passing shot on the run as Davidovich Fokina took the semi-final to a deciding set.

Zverev was broken to trail 2-0 in the third set and could not recover control of the tie as the 25th seed advanced to his first ATP Masters 1000 final since 2022.
