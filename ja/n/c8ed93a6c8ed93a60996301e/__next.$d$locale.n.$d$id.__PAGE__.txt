SINGAPORE: Red Bull's Max Verstappen tasted victory in Singapore for the first time as the polesitter won a chaotic sprint on Saturday (Oct 10) after the start was delayed by over 30 minutes due to heavy rain while nine cars retired, a record for sprint races.

The victory was the first of any kind for the Dutchman in Singapore as he took the chequered flag ahead of Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc.

"I want to win tomorrow. I don't see this as a win in terms of actually winning a Grand Prix," Verstappen said.

Mercedes' championship leader Kimi Antonelli was gifted fourth place after the two McLarens collided on the final lap while his teammate George Russell crashed out of the lead on lap one.

The race was the first Formula 1 sprint race to be held in Singapore.
