Friday night's fifth-place play-off attracted an attendance of just 32,000 with the uppermost tier of the 82,000-capacity stadium entirely closed.

Ireland beat the Wallabies 38-14 after Italy overcame Fiji on the first day of the inaugural competition's finals day weekend at the home of England rugby.

Ticket sales for Saturday and Sunday's action, which are headlined by England v New Zealand and the final between South Africa and France, have been stronger, but Farrell questioned the Friday night double-bill.

"I'd love to spend some time to have a think about it, I've been preparing a team and you're busy doing that," he said.

"But off the top of my head, I don't know whether the Friday night thing works or being at the same venue. If we'd have played at the Aviva (Stadium in Dublin), it would have been full.
