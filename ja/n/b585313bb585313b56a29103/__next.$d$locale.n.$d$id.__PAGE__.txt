The attacks come amid little progress in talks to end the nearly five-year-old war and after Ukraine, on three occasions this week, struck the Kerch bridge linking Russian-occupied Crimea with Russia.

Russian President Vladimir Putin had vowed a "painful" response to the Kerch attack, as the bridge is vital for war supplies for Russian forces in the south.

Among the dead in Friday evening's double-tap strikes on Kryvyi Rih were a 7-year-old boy and a 20-year-old woman, while several children were among the 60 wounded, the Ukrainian officials said.

Several fires broke out and damage was caused to about 25 apartment buildings, a hospital, schools and an industrial facility, Oleksandr Vilkul, the head of the city administration, wrote on Telegram.

Moscow, meanwhile, confirmed a further strike on Friday night in Kryvyi Rih.

Russia's Defense Ministry said a thermal power plant in the city was hit, along with a power substation in the region of Rivne, on the opposite side of the country.
