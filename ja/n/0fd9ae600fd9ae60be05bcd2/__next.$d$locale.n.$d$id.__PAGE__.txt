5:T4eb,Nobel Peace Prize awarded to human rights lawyer Navi Pillay South African human rights lawyer Navi Pillay wins the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize for advancing human rights and international law.

South African lawyer Navanethem "Navi" Pillay sits at the Nuremberg Forum in Germany following the announcement that she will be awarded the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize. The Nobel Committee is honoring her for her efforts to promote peace and international law.

Navanethem "Navi" Pillay, a leading South African jurist and former U.N. human rights chief, won the Nobel Peace Prize on Friday for her lifetime commitment to international law.

At a ceremony in Oslo, Norwegian Nobel Committee Chair Jørgen Watne Frydnes called Pillay "one of the most courageous and important legal experts of our time."

"There is a thread to her involvement in standing and supporting Nelson Mandela and the prisoners of Robben Island, to her being a key to all the major international court cases of our time," he said.

The 85-year-old was attending a conference in Nuremberg, Germany, when she received the news.

At a news conference, Pillay said she was deeply honored and dedicated the prize to "all of the survivors of international crimes" and "defenders of international justice."d:X
