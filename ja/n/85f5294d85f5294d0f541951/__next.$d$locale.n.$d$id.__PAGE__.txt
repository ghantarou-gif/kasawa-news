Two former U.S service members who were stationed at Pearl Harbor in Hawaii when Japan attacked in 1941 died on the same day last week, it has been learned.

Gebhard (Geb) Galle, died on Saturday at his home in Moses Lake, Wash., according to his daughter, Lynda Palmer. He was 105.

Galle died the same day as the oldest known Pearl Harbor survivor, Freeman Johnson, of Centerville, Mass., who was 106.
