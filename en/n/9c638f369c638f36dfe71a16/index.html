Pope Leo leaves hospital for Vatican four days after lung cancer surgery

5 hours ago

71-year-old Pope Leo left hospital on Saturday to return to the Vatican. ( Reuters: Vincenzo Livieri )

Pope Leo has returned to the Vatican from hospital.

It comes after the 71-year old underwent surgery for lung cancer four days ago.

The pope's doctors said he could return home following his latest blood tests and chest imaging.

Pope Leo XIV has left hospital in Rome to return to the Vatican following surgery for lung cancer four days ago.

The 71-year-old walked unaided to his waiting car, stopping to speak to well-wishers outside Rome's Gemelli Hospital on Saturday afternoon.

Pope Leo walks ahead of Stefano Margaritora, his lead surgeon. ( Reuters: Yara Nardi )

His doctors had earlier said he was well enough to return home after they carried out the latest blood tests and chest imaging.
