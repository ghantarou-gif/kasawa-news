Serbia: Tens of thousands rally ahead of elections

Oct 23, 03:15 AM

Over a hundred thousand of Serbians took to the streets in support of Serbia 's protesting youth movement on Thursday, in what was the final campaign rally ahead of a pivotal election challenge to the Balkan nation's populist strongman, Aleksandar Vucic .

Crowds poured into Belgrade's central square from all directions and filled up several blocks around the area. Independent crowd-monitoring group Archive of Public Gatherings estimated about 150,000 people were at the rally.

The optimistic crowds chanted "victory" ahead of Sunday's crunch vote which comes after two years of student-led protests against Vucic. It was triggered by a 2024 collapse of a canopy at the newly renovated Novi Sad railway station which killed 16 people.

The anti-corruption movement, led by university students, has since snowballed from demands for an independent investigation into the incident, into calls for early elections.

The Student List, a leaderless movement of student-backed candidates, is the veteran leader's biggest threat.

Thousands supporting it flocked to Belgrade's streets on Thursday.

"I came to support the Student List because young people finally gave me hope that it is possible to live in a normal country," 47-year-old Marija Nedeljkovic Radic told the French AFP news agency as she arrived for the students' rally.
