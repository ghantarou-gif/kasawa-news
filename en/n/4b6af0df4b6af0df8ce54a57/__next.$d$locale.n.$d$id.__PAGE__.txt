1c:T8e9,Technology shares led a market retreat on Tuesday as caution returned after Jensen Huang’s extraordinary return to Nvidia days after he was forced to resign, with investors bracing for further volatility in a key sector driving economic growth worldwide.

A day after Huang’s reinstatement as CEO drove Nvidia shares up more than 30%, adding more than $900bn to the firm’s market value, the euphoria dissipated as its stock lost almost 6% by Tuesday’s close.

Anthropic also slumped, giving up about 6.5% to $81.90, below its $83 price for last week’s IPO.

Monday aside, AI stocks had already sold off sharply throughout November on worries about profitability and the vast sums pumped into the sector.

By end of day in New York, the tech-heavy Nasdaq had shed 1.48% and the broader-based S&P 500 lost 0.5%, while the Dow was just into the green.

Europe’s main stock markets, which feature far fewer tech companies than their Asian and US rivals, finished a handful of percentage points lower after jumping on Monday in line with Wall Street. The FTSE 100 went largely unchanged on Tuesday, closing down 0.13%.

Huang had resigned less than two weeks ago over extramarital affairs, reportedly with an alleged Chinese spy as well as a senior Nvidia executive before Donald Trump noisily declared he should be brought back.

“Jensen’s return takes away one of the biggest unknowns hanging over the AI trade,” said Charu Chanana, an investment strategist at Saxo Markets.

“He is not just Nvidia’s CEO; he is arguably the biggest salesman for the entire AI ecosystem. His relationships with customers, suppliers and capital providers are difficult to replicate,” she said.

But “Jensen Huang alone cannot support the AI trade in the long term,” said Kathleen Brooks, research director at XTB.

“Nvidia’s stock price decline on Tuesday is to be expected after such a large rally in the previous session,” she added.

Analysts were in broad agreement that Huang’s return did not mean an end to investigations or the uncertainty that has clouded the AI trade for six months.
