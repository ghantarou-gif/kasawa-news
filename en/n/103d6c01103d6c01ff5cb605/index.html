How to find your perfect pillow - according to an expert

Sep 28, 09:45 AM

"I am one of those people," says Dr Chris McCarthy. "I do take my pillow with me."

You may have seen those people at the airport - a pillow stuffed under an arm as they drag their suitcases to check-in.

Or you might be one yourself - attached to a pillow that's soft enough to settle into but robust enough that it's not bunched into a ball by morning.

McCarthy insists he's not being fussy. He's a top physiotherapist and knows the wrong pillow can be a pain in the neck.

He doesn't take his abroad, but as soon as he gets to his hotel he starts "faffing about" with the pillows - and he's got a tried and tested trick to make even the most lacklustre one fit for sleep. Here's his guide to choosing the perfect pillow for you.

What makes the perfect pillow? One advertised for your sleeping position? Soft or firm? Feathers, foam or fibre?

Sliced Bread separates science fact from the marketing fiction.

McCarthy, an associate professor at the Manchester School of Physiotherapy, says the right pillow will depend on what position you sleep in.
