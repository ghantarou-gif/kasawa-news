29:T21fc,Leonida is so huge as to be overwhelming and in the opening hours, the game bombards you with tonnes of info that you will inevitably forget. But I’ve done nothing but play this game for weeks and I’m here to help. Here’s what I wished I’d known at the start

Walk faster. I found that Jason and Lucia walk incredibly slowly. If you go into the options menu, you can select “fast walk” as the default walking speed, which sped them up just a little without ruining the pace or atmosphere of the game. While you’re in there, you can also customise plenty of other things, including controls and button mapping.

Don’t worry too much about money. In the opening chapters of the game – which could take you 20 or more hours to play through – it can feel like everything is expensive and there’s never enough cash. But later on, you will have many more ways to make money and a $200 cab ride will feel like nothing. This problem will fix itself, so in the meantime, just enjoy yourself.

Bank your cash at ATMs. If you get busted by the cops or killed by anyone, you will lose a lot of your cash – and anything you had in your stash bags, too. Trouble can start at any time in Vice City, so it’s not a great idea to walk around with holdalls full of drugs and pockets full of money. Use the BuckMe app on your phone to find your nearest ATM and bank your cash, so it remains safe. And you can stash those holdalls in the trunk of your car.

Do not use your own vehicle for anything illegal . If your car is hanging around near a crime scene and the cops identify it, it’ll get impounded, which is a massive pain because you have to steal it back. Instead, just hotwire a getaway car and have it parked nearby. Then you can abandon it later with impunity.

Choose your battles … Grand Theft Auto VI. Photograph: Rockstar

Get your partner to fence your goods. If you cannot be bothered to drive all the way to a fence, you can hand your stash bags to your partner and ask them to do it before returning to you. You might get a little less money this way, but it saves repetitive drives and conversations. Later in the game you can also ask your fences to pick stuff up from stash boxes in your owned garages.

During missions, make liberal use of the “retry from checkpoint” option. Outside of story missions, you have to deal with the consequences of your blunders in GTA VI, whether you miss your highway exit on a long drive, pull out a gun at the wrong moment in a restaurant and start a mini gang war, or mess up a robbery and get busted. But inside the missions? You can neatly erase all your problems by pausing the game and selecting “retry checkpoint”. Take a wrong turn? Retry checkpoint. Shot the wrong person? Retry checkpoint. Accidentally run someone over? Retry checkpoint. Ended up miles from where you’re supposed to be after escaping the cops? Retry checkpoint, it usually gets you closer. This saved me a lot of time and frustration, and there was still quite enough unpredictable chaos and self-inflicted disaster to deal with outside of the story missions.

Don’t kill anyone unless you have to. Surprisingly you can get a lot of crime done in GTA VI without doing much killing at all (outside of story missions where you have to shoot a zillion bad guys/pursuing cops). On robberies and stick-ups you’re rewarded for stealth. You can sometimes sneak in unnoticed through back doors and avoid security entirely. But if that’s not an option – if a security guard starts shooting instead of surrendering when you point a gun at them, for instance – then non-lethal solutions attract less trouble from the cops. You can shoot an arm or leg to disable someone rather than killing them, or knock them out with the butt of a gun. All of this will make your getaway easier and make you feel less guilty.

Understand how heat works. Crime is a little more complex in GTA VI. Your wanted level is still indicated by stars, and this is directly related to the seriousness of your crime. Stealing a car is one star, robbing a store is three or more, killing cops always adds another star. The police response escalates with your wanted level, determining whether they send out helicopters or set up roadblocks to stop you. But now there’s also “heat”: in areas where you’ve previously committed a crime, the cops will be looking for you. They’ll have a description of your car and your outfit. A simple thing you can do is always put on a mask and a hat before you do anything that might attract the cops – then you can simply remove them afterwards. It’s also wise to change vehicles once you’re out of sight of witnesses and police. Changing outfits is more difficult: you have to store them in the trunk of your own car or buy new ones – but I found that changing cars and wearing masks was usually enough.

Stay redeemable … Grand Theft Auto VI. Photograph: Rockstar

Watch your criminal profile. GTA VI tracks your actions as both Jason and Lucia and gives them a criminal profile. Unnecessary violence such as running people over or shooting bystanders will quickly turn you from “professional” or “hardened” to “violent” or “psycho”, which has serious repercussions for the game’s story and ending. If you go too far then either character can become “irredeemable”, and there’s no way back. You can get your criminal profile back into the professional range by being as non-lethal as possible and leaving innocent bystanders alone.

Expect people to fight back . This is Leonida: pedestrians are often packing. If you start behaving badly or insulting people on the street, someone might throw a punch or pull a gun on you. Drive into someone’s car and they might have an episode of road rage and spent the next few minutes chasing you down and trying to run you off the road. This is all extremely fun – insulting people on the street sometimes leads to some hilarious back-and-forth exchanges – but if you’re trying to stay out of trouble, don’t mess with the locals.

Prepare for big thefts . You can get away with a simple liquor store smash’n’grab by running in, waving your gun about and then getting outta there, but later in the game, if you want to rob anywhere with a real return – a bank, or a big jewellery store – then you need to have a plan for when the police inevitably turn up. Park your car somewhere quiet and fill the trunk with a change of clothes, so that you can return to it after the job, stash your loot and weapons in the trunk, and disguise yourself. If you’re clever with this you can walk or drive right past the cops after a job, safe in the knowledge that you won’t be recognised. Parking your own car in a parking garage is an especially clever option, as helicopters can’t see you in there.

Rob the criminals instead. I wasn’t far into this game before I started to feel bad about terrorising small business owners for money, but you don’t technically have to do much of this. You can make just as much money by robbing other criminals. Leonida is full of gang hangouts that you can easily infiltrate, full of drugs and cash that you can steal. The risk is that gang members will shoot you on sight if they see you skulking about, but helpfully, that also makes me feel less bad about shooting back.

It goes deep … Grand Theft Auto VI. Photograph: Rockstar

Know when to cut your losses. Whether you’re robbing a store or trying to steal cocaine from under the noses of terrifying biker gangs, things can go south very fast in GTA. Sometimes the best idea is just to get outta there and avoid losing whatever you’ve gained.

Sometimes it’s easier to get away on foot. If the cops are really chasing you down, with armoured cars and helicopters, it can be very difficult to get away in a car. Sometimes the best thing you can do is get out and run away on foot, making use of alleyways and covered parking garages in the city and tree cover out in the countryside. I once spent a good 15 minutes failing to get away from the police on the highway, crashing three cars in the process, before diving into a river and swimming desperately to the other side, ending up hiding on a golf course with $100k in stolen bills.

Take your time. This truly is an extraordinarily massive game and it will take weeks to get through even if you play for several hours a day. If you’re enjoying yourself, don’t feel like you have to rush to make progress. I found every chapter of this game to be enjoyable in its own way, and they all feel different from each other.
