Varma leads India fightback in four-wicket victory over New Zealand

40 minutes ago

Add CNA as a trusted source to help Google better understand and surface our content in search results.

Oct 24 : India's middle-order batter Tilak Varma hammered a half-century off 25 balls as India chased down a target of 181 with a ball to spare, taking a 2-0 lead in the five-match Twenty20 series with New Zealand on Saturday.

New Zealand captain Mitchell Santner's destructive spell (4-21) had left India shaken, with 68 runs needed from the last five overs. But Varma (51) and Nitish Kumar Reddy (26) targeted the pacers in the final three overs to secure a four-wicket win.

"The Hyderabadi boys were in play today ... what everyone believed, I have done it, so I'm really proud of myself," said player-of-the-match Varma, who partnered with Reddy to add 69 runs off 30 balls for the seventh wicket.

The Christchurch pitch looked slower than in Thursday's high-scoring contest as India's pacers restricted New Zealand to 43-3 during the powerplay, but Glenn Phillips (70) and Bevon Jacobs (58) turned the innings around, adding 116 for the fourth wicket.

Phillips scored his second straight fifty as he hit four boundaries and four sixes before Arshdeep Singh (2-33) got him caught near the fence. Michael Bracewell then scored 10 off two balls to help the hosts reach 180-5.
