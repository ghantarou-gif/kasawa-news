28:T12af,Apple’s base-model MacBook Pro gets a solid speed bump, offering most of the features from its top-level siblings but in a design that has just been superseded and may soon be phased out.

It is the starting model in Apple’s popular MacBook Pro line, but that doesn’t make it cheap. Costing from £1,999 (€2,299/$1,999/A$3,199) it is £300 or equivalent more than its predecessor as RAMageddon continues to jack up prices. It is, at least, £500 cheaper than the other new MacBook Pros.

The laptop’s solid aluminium body, look and feel are identical to its predecessors, using the design first introduced in 2021 . With five generations of the machine having been and gone, it is a bit of a known quantity, but the 120Hz miniLED screen is still brilliant, as are the first-class keyboard and trackpad. The port selection is also solid and the MagSafe charging cable remains a delight.

The MacBook Pro has a best-in-class keyboard and trackpad, which makes using it a satisfying experience. Photograph: Samuel Gibbs/The Guardian

The M6 model only comes with the smaller 14.2in screen, which is still the sweet spot for portability and productivity. It reaches a sustained 1,000nits brightness for standard content and working – double that of the MacBook Air – and hits 1,600nits to make HDR content pop. It is only really beaten by the OLED screen on the new MacBook Pro design , which costs considerably more.

Screen: 14.2in mini LED (3024x1964; 254 ppi) ProMotion (120Hz)

Operating system: macOS 26.4 Tahoe

Connectivity: wifi 7, Bluetooth 6, Thread, 3x Thunderbolt 4/USB 4, HDMI 2.1, SDXC, headphones

Dimensions: 221.2 x 312.6 x 15.5mm

The MacBook Pro has three USB-C ports, an HDMI port, a headphones socket, an SD card slot and the MagSafe charging port. Photograph: Samuel Gibbs/The Guardian

The M6 is Apple’s latest generation chip and was first used in the Mac mini to great effect . Compared with the M5 generation of chips, including the current MacBook Air , the M6 MacBook Pro is about 11% faster for day-to-day usage and 24% for more intensive multi-core tasks. The extra performance wasn’t that noticeable in day-to-day tasks, but j ust like the M5 version , it absolutely flies during use.

However, it also has up to 57% faster graphics performance, beating many of Apple’s older M-Pro chips. That makes the M6 quite a leap from older machines with more than enough power for many creative work tasks.

The MacBook Pro also has fantastic battery life, lasting about three days of light work between charges using the browser, messaging, notes and word processing apps. That makes it one of the longest-lasting laptops for general use, but even for more intensive work, developing and editing photos, the battery still lasted about two days.

The recycled aluminium body of the MacBook Pro is one of the very best in the industry: solid, smooth and looks great. Photograph: Samuel Gibbs/The Guardian

The MacBook Pro is made of 45% recycled materials, which Apple breaks down in its environmental impact report . The battery should last in excess of 1,000 full-charge cycles and can be replaced for £225 by Apple. Apple makes repair manuals available . The company offers trade-in and free recycling schemes.

The MacBook Pro M6 costs from £1,999 (€2,299/ $1,999 / A$3,199 ).

For comparison, the 14in M5 Pro MacBook Pro costs £2,499 , the 14in OLED MacBook Pro costs from £2,899 , the 13in MacBook Air costs £1,299 , the Dell XPS 14 costs from £2,038.45 and the Microsoft Surface Laptop Ultra costs £2,599 .

The M6 MacBook Pro is a potent machine for general use that is let down by its high price, mostly thanks to the scourge of RAMageddon .

But it is also likely one of the last iterations of its design, having been superseded by the new thinner, lighter and OLED-equipped MacBook Pro.

The M6 is a great laptop with fantastically long battery life and a quality screen, keyboard and trackpad, built on the foundations of the many great MacBook Pros over the last five years.

But at £1,999 or equivalent it is also a difficult pill to swallow, primarily aimed at Apple buyers who want a nicer screen or more ports than a MacBook Air and are prepared to pay £700 more for it. For everyone else, there are better models available with more grunt or a cheaper price.

Pros: great performance from M6 chip, minimum of 16GB memory and 1TB storage, super-long battery life, great ProMotion screen, great Centre Stage camera, plenty of ports and an SD card slot, great speakers, Touch ID, top-class keyboard and trackpad, wifi 7, Bluetooth 6 and Thread support.
