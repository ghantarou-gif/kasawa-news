Trump wants to dismantle the ICC. These jurists want Canada to intervene

5 hours ago

After the Trump administration announced sweeping sanctions against the International Criminal Court, Canadian legal professionals are calling on the federal government to take action to strengthen the court through additional funding or other measures.

The International Criminal Court is seen in The Hague, in the Netherlands, last year. The United States announced on Friday it is putting new sanctions on the court. (Peter Dejong/The Associated Press)

When Payam Akhavan talks about the Trump administration's new, sweeping sanctions on the International Criminal Court, the first feeling he brings up is despair.

"These institutions, which were once sacrosanct, are besieged," said Akhavan, a Canadian who was appointed as a special adviser on genocide at the ICC in 2021.

The United States government, in the words of U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio, is now working to "dismantle the ICC, piece by piece."

But Akhavan says it's not too late to stand up for the court.

"Canada is at a historical juncture where it has to choose which side of history it's going to be on," he said.
