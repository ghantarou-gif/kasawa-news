Israel's Netanyahu proposes rival Eisenkot serve as first PM in rotating premiership

13 minutes ago

Israeli caretaker Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu on Saturday offered to make way for opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot, saying his rival could take office first if the two agree to alternate as prime minister. Netanyahu said threats to the country's very existence justified dropping the custom that the biggest party leads the government.
