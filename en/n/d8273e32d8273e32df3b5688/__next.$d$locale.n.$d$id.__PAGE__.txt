Busfield, a director and producer of The Cleaning Lady, was charged in February in a four-count grand jury indictment that accused the actor of groping one of the ‌boys.

He steadfastly professed his innocence, calling the allegations against him “lies”, and his attorneys asked for the charges against him to be dismissed.

“The prosecuting attorney failed ​to act in a ​fair and impartial manner,” Montano wrote, ​adding that the lawyer’s actions “had the practical effect of unilaterally withholding witnesses ​requested by the ‌grand jurors”.
